Setup In Minutes

Connect. Assign. Control.

Three intuitive steps to full operational visibility and hard spend limits over every AI tool across your enterprise.

STEP 01

Connect Your AI Providers

Paste in your API keys for any provider — Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, Bedrock, Mistral, Groq, or custom endpoints. OneOps immediately vaults them with zero-knowledge AES-256 encryption.

🔐 vault.encrypt("sk-ant-...", provider="anthropic")
STEP 02

Create Teams & Assign Models

Organize users into teams and assign specific AI models with point-and-click ease. Define monthly hard budget caps per person or team. Automatically sync roles via Okta or Azure AD SCIM.

👥 teams.assign(name="Dev", models=["Claude-4", "Bedrock"])
STEP 03

Track, Alert & Optimize

Monitor spend and token burn in real time. Receive proactive alerts before budget thresholds are hit. OneOps blocks prompt PII leakage and generates instant audit chargebacks.

⚡ alert.threshold(burn="80%", notify=["#finance-slack"])
